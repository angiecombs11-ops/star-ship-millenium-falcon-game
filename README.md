# Starling Academy

Starling Academy is a dependency-free browser-game prototype for kindergarten
through second-grade reading and math practice.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this folder:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8000`.

## Core loop

1. Select Kindergarten, Grade 1, or Grade 2.
2. Move two or three cadets aboard the Millennium Falcon.
3. Launch a five-question reading and math mission.
4. Earn navigation stars and replay with another crew or grade.

Each grade includes 16 challenges. Consecutive missions avoid the five
questions from the previous flight, and the ship advances along its route
after every answer.

## Controls

- Mouse: click controls or drag cadets onto the ship and back to the crew dock.
- Touchscreen: tap controls or drag cadets with a finger.
- Game controller: use the D-pad or left stick to move focus, A/Cross to
  choose, B/Circle to go back, and Start/Menu to launch or replay.

The ship, setting, and cadets are original, space-adventure-inspired designs
that can be expanded without relying on licensed characters or branding.
